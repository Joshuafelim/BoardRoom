MINISTRY DIRECTIVES

1. The Statement of Comprehensive Income (SCI) reveals operational profitability.

  • Gross Profit = Revenue - Cost of Sales.
  • Operating Profit (EBIT) = Gross Profit - Selling Expenses - Admin Expenses.
  • Net Profit = Operating Profit - Finance Costs - Taxation.

2. Statement of Financial Position (Balance Sheet) reveals structural health & integrity.

  • Total Assets = Non-current Assets + Current Assets.
  • Total Liabilities = Current Liabilities + Non-current Liabilities.
  • THE GOLDEN RULE: Net Assets (Assets - Liabilities) MUST equal Total Equity.
  • IDENTITY RULE: Company names and registrations must match across documents!

3. Verdict Guidelines & Fraud Types

  • APPROVE: Math is correct, company is profitable, solvent, and records match.
  • DENY: Math is genuine, but company is insolvent (Current Liabilities > Current Assets) or has sustained losses.
  • INSPECT (AUDIT): Fraud detected! Look out for math errors, omitted tax lines, inverted signs (e.g. adding costs), name mismatches across statements, or unauthorized offshore stamps. Tap the suspect row/header in Inspect mode!
