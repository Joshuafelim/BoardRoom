MINISTRY DIRECTIVES

1. The Statement of Comprehensive Income (SCI) reveals operational profitability.

  • Gross Profit = Revenue - Cost of Sales. (The purest measure of trading efficiency).
  • Operating Profit (EBIT) = Gross Profit - Selling Expenses - Admin Expenses.
  • Profit Before Tax = Operating Profit - Finance Costs.
  • Net Profit (Profit for Year) = Profit Before Tax - Taxation.

2. The Statement of Financial Position (Balance Sheet) reveals structural health and liquidity.

  • Total Assets = Non-current Assets + Current Assets.
  • Total Liabilities = Current Liabilities + Non-current Liabilities.
  • Net Assets = Total Assets - Total Liabilities.
  • Total Equity = Share Capital + Retained Earnings.
  • THE GOLDEN RULE: Net Assets MUST exactly equal Total Equity.

3. Verdict Guidelines

  • APPROVE: Company math is correct, profitable, and solvent (Current Assets > Current Liabilities).
  • DENY: Company math is correct, but they are structurally failing (Sustained Net Loss) OR insolvent (Current Liabilities > Current Assets, creating Negative Working Capital).
  • INSPECT (AUDIT): The company is lying. A calculation on the sheet is mathematically false. Activate Inspect mode and click the false row to report fraud.
